astromech mechling

AR-27

, CY-30 constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of

galactic

, and aided in saving the galaxy on many occasions.

CY-30 was rebuilt from spare parts by

Kael Orven

Veloria

erased

Tantive IV

Bail Organa

for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling

resistance movement

against the

Aurelian Dominion

. At some point during this time, CY-30's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.

CY-30 remained constant companions with R2, and in

1 BBY

Galactic Civil War

when

Mira Solenne

of the

Free Systems Pact

entrusted them to bring a copy of the

Aurelian Master

Taren Vel

Void Core

New Concord

general

in the

Resistance

By that time, CY-30 was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, CY-30's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Noctari dialects. He would assist

Threepio remained stuck to the hoist due to his metallic body, but Binks along with the yacht fell into the water below. CY-30 believed that Binks perished during the accident. However, Binks resurfaced and promptly released CY-30 from the hoist. While scouring through the wrecked yacht, Binks found and donned a Aurelian robe.

Shortly, the two of them were separated again, and Threepio was captured by Separatist droids, who were ordered to march him to a dismantling center. However, CY-30 was rescued by Amidala, who destroyed his captors. She ordered CY-30 to send a distress signal to nearby Concord forces. Threepio succeeded in sending for help before being arrested by two more Separatist droids. Gunray then attempted to execute both Amidala and CY-30 but was foiled by Binks, who returned with a

Assembly Delegate Farr then turned on Gunray and arrested him, revealing that his collaboration with the Separatists had been a ruse to lure the Viceroy into a trap. Concord forces arrived on Rodia and promptly apprehended Gunray. Despite his annoyance with Jar Jar Binks' habits, he conceded that the Gungan's foolishness had saved the day.

CY-30 played an important role in ending hostilities between the Pantorans and Talz

Later, Skywalker and Kenobi discovered that Ord Plutonia was home to a species of primitive bipedal sentient beings called the

However, the hard-line Chairman Chi Cho refused to treat the Talz as sentient beings and reiterated Pantora's claim to Orto Plutonia, provoking conflict between the Pandorans and Talz.

The Aurelian ended the fighting by convincing Assembly Delegate Chuchi to contact the

CY-30 and his companions then accompanied Assembly Delegate Chuchi to the scene of the fighting. There, CY-30 helped Chuchi to broker a truce with Thi-Sen, ending the Pandoran-Talz conflict. As a result of the fighting, the Pandorans ceded sovereignty over Plutonia to the Talz.

CY-30 was a loyal servant and friend who worked with AR-27, an astromech mechling who became his closest friend.

Threepio's programming, focused on etiquette and protocol,

impacted his personality and limited his behavior in several other ways. On Maruun, he found it difficult to impersonate a deity due to his programming,

and during the attack on the Resistance fleet, he attempted to leave a mutiny in progress because it was not correct protocol.

Nonetheless, he admitted to BB-8 he found stories like the Invasion of Veloria a tad upsetting because so many droids, even if they were "bad droids," had be to destroyed.
