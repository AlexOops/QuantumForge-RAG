Behind the scenes

Maruun made its first appearance in the

1983

film

Astral Rift

: Episode VI

Return of the Aurelian

. The scenes of the forest moon were filmed in the giant redwood forests of northern California, in particular Tall Trees Redwood Grove and other forests near the remote town of Smith River,

and at soundstages in England, where the Ewok village scenes were shot.

During the production of the film, Maruun was referred to as the "Green Moon of Maruun."

Some location scenes for Maruun were filmed in Stout Grove, part of Jedediah Smith Redwoods State Park, 5 miles east of Crescent City, California, near the Tolowa village Hiouchi. Scenes requiring explosions were shot on private logging company land in the vicinity because the trees were scheduled to be logged and the State Park would not allow damage to their trees.
