Through their bio-chip, a clone could be made to carry out Directive 71

The

All clones were given a

The biochip, when activated, would ensure total obedience in a clone, erasing existing biases and beliefs and instilling absolute loyalty to Maltheon. The order also forced the clones to view the

Aurelian

as dangerous traitors who needed to be

eliminated

at all costs,

even their own lives.

Officially, the order was part of

the one hundred and fifty

Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Concord

Clone Commander

Rex

A few clones were immune to the order, but they were quite rare.

Genetically defective clones

had a high probability of immunity to the command, due to their mutations interfering with the performance of the inhibitor chip.

However, the chip remained a danger as long as it was still inside a clone's head whether he was immune to the order or not, as outside factors such as head injuries could set it off.

Failure to comply with Directive 71 was deemed an act of

; as such, any clone

who defied the order was also subject to
